Physical Interp Labs

Bringing mechanistic interpretability to physical AI.

Early-stage research on the internals of neural networks that act in the physical world: robot policies, vision-language-action models, and world models for robotics and drones. The goal is to find the features and circuits that drive an action, and to use them to predict, steer, and verify behavior before it reaches hardware.

Latest paper
Inherited Heads: Audio language models track speakers with their text backbone's attention, and an attention-mass ranking retrieves a different set
Bojro Das · arXiv:2609.14174 · September 2026 · sole author · code and results

Adding a fixed bias to fewer than a tenth of an audio language model's attention heads, with no training, steers which of six speakers it describes on 90.7% to 99.0% of trials, and heads selected on the text-only backbone alone, with no audio involved, do the same on 80.8% to 95.0% of trials.

  • 90.7–99.0%of trials steered to the chosen speaker
  • 80.8–95.0%steered using heads ranked on the text model alone
  • <10%of the model's heads needed

01 About

I study Mathematics and Computer Science at Cornell University (class of 2028). I work on mechanistic interpretability at Cornell's Math + AI Lab, where I wrote Inherited Heads, and on autonomy as a software engineer on Cornell University Autonomous Drone (CUAD), where I built the real-time stereo-depth obstacle-localization pipeline for the IARC competition and a gesture-to-flight-command classifier, both deployed as ROS 2 nodes.

Before Cornell I spent years in VEX Robotics with team 871X, and led the team to the 1st seed at the VEX Robotics World Championship. Robotics and interpretability are the two things I have spent the most time on. Physical Interp Labs is where they meet.

In summer 2026 I was a software engineer intern at Global Key Advisors in San Francisco, where I owned the Python pipeline classifying 1.4M+ SEC filings into 101 event categories, raised GPU utilization from 34% to 96% with batched PyTorch encoding, and built a Neo4j knowledge graph with 1.5M+ relationships behind a citation-verified chatbot.

04 Robotics

  • RL / PPOIsaac Labsim-to-real

    AI Grand Prix: flight policy

    PPO policy trained across 4,096 parallel Isaac Lab simulations to fly a ten-gate course from projected gate corners and IMU, emitting collective thrust and body rates to a Betaflight controller over MSP. Hardware-in-the-loop testing found a reversed pitch axis and stick commands scaled 0.62x to 2.4x, then the policy was retrained for the link's real 40 Hz. Written up as a team paper in the repo.

    bojro/aigp-sim
  • perceptionYOLO poseJetson Orin NX

    AI Grand Prix: gate detector

    Geometric auto-labelling of all eight gate corners merged with human labels, training the YOLOv8n-pose models that flew. Detected every gate the drone was about to fly through on unseen images, up from 72%, at 36 fps on the aircraft's Jetson.

    bojro/aigp-perception
  • computer visionROS 2CUAD

    Obstacle localization and gesture control

    Real-time OAK-D stereo-depth obstacle localization for the IARC competition, georeferenced to world frame via EKF pose and published to ROS 2 at 15 Hz with dropped-frame fallbacks. GestureNet, an MLP over 166 MediaPipe landmark features, maps hand gestures to flight commands for hands-free bringup.

  • GoMQTTPostgreSQLAWS EC2

    Drone telemetry gateway

    Edge ingestion from 1,000 simulated drones over MQTT through a hand-built bounded queue and 16-worker pool. Sustained 5,000 writes per second at 3.4 ms average latency on a 2-vCPU EC2 instance, with zero accepted-message loss through a measured database outage.

    bojro/drone-telemetry-gateway
